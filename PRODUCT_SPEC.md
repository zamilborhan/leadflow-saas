# LeadFlow BD

## Product
Bangladesh-focused Facebook Lead Management and WhatsApp Follow-up SaaS.

## Target Customers
- Coaching centers
- IELTS centers
- Education consultancies
- Real estate businesses
- Visa consultancies

## Main Problem
Businesses receive Facebook leads but lose potential customers because:
- leads are not organized
- sales agents forget follow-ups
- leads are not assigned properly
- WhatsApp follow-up is inconsistent
- campaign-to-sale performance is unclear

## Core Solution
LeadFlow captures Facebook leads automatically, stores them in CRM,
assigns leads to sales agents, enables WhatsApp follow-up,
creates reminders, and tracks conversion.

## MVP
1. Authentication
2. Business workspace
3. Team members
4. Lead CRM
5. Lead assignment
6. Notes
7. Follow-up reminders
8. Facebook Lead Ads integration
9. WhatsApp Cloud API integration
10. Dashboard
11. Subscription billing
12. Admin panel
